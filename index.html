01

Research

Our aim is to develop novel therapeutic modalities for human diseases through genome editing and gene therapy. Four threads run through the lab.

Prediction

Deep learning for editing outcomes

Models that predict the efficiency and precise outcome of Cas nucleases, base editors, and prime editors from sequence alone — DeepSpCas9, DeepCpf1, DeepBE, DeepPrime and the DeepGE suite.

Engineering

Better editors

High-fidelity, high-activity variants such as Sniper2L, and prime editors boosted by DNA-repair biology (hyPE2 with the Rad51 domain), built by elucidating how cells process an edit.

Function

Reading every variant

Saturation genome editing with prime and base editors to assign function and drug resistance to clinical variants — ATM, ABL1, EGFR, ALK, and the androgen receptor.

Therapy

In vivo gene correction

Base and prime editing delivered in living animals — virus-like particles and non-viral routes — to treat inherited diseases of the eye, liver, and ear.
